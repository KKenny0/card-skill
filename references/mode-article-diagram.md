# Article diagram: personal relations

Use [personal relation layouts](article-diagram-personal.md) and `schemas/article-diagram.json`. Explicitly select exchange, convergence, tension, cycle, timeline or hierarchy from source semantics. Fixed 3:2; measured labels; no shrinking to fit. Split by responsibility when outside limits.

Legacy family, summary, auto and split formula-card routes are retired. Formula text may be retained as a relationship heading where supported by the source, but no formula-only output or invented graph is allowed. See [migration](personal-style-migration.md).
